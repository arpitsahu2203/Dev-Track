Why Random Problem Grinding Fails.

Most software engineers approach technical interview preparation by opening a problem list and solving 300 questions at random. Three weeks later, 80% of the approaches have faded from memory.

Cognitive psychology has consistently shown that retrieval practice and spaced repetition trump sheer exposure. Dev Tracker is engineered around this single fundamental truth.

I

Immediacy Over Volume

The 5 minutes immediately after solving an algorithm is when your mind understands the subtle invariants, edge-case constraints, and state transitions best. Logging takes 20 seconds and preserves that insight forever.

II

Asymptotic Precision

Interviewers test whether you can reason about memory limits and worst-case time bounds. Dev Tracker pairs every logged problem with AI complexity verification so you know exactly why O(N) beats O(N²).

III

Active Recall Checkpoints

Re-reading a solution is passive recognition—it tricks you into feeling competent. True test readiness requires generating the approach from scratch. Dev Tracker’s AI self-quiz ensures active recall before interviews.
