About Dev Tracker

Practice deliberately. Revise with confidence.

Dev Tracker exists for developers who want their DSA practice to be more than a growing solved count. It is a quiet place to turn completed problems into knowledge you can use again.

Why it exists

Unstructured practice makes strong preparation harder.

A useful solution can disappear into browser history. A difficult topic can remain invisible in a long list of solved questions. And when an interview approaches, it should be easy to find exactly what needs another pass.

Keep the context

Links, platforms, difficulty, topics, attempts, and your own notes stay connected.

See your balance

Review the distribution of practice instead of relying on memory.

Return with purpose

Mark questions for revision and return to the original details quickly.

Build a habit

A small, consistent record makes deliberate practice easier to sustain.

Product philosophy

Clarity over clutter.

Dev Tracker is built around information that helps you decide what to do next. It avoids invented scores and distractions, and instead gives your existing practice a reliable structure.

Focused

The workspace puts your problems and revision information first.

Reflective

AI review is there to support revision, not replace thoughtful practice.

Personal

Your preparation record belongs to your own learning journey.

Built for the long path to mastery.

Whether you are starting DSA practice, preparing for an interview, or returning to fundamentals, organize your work around what you want to remember.
